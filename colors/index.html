House of Colour · tap a colour for full screen

For true colours, check your screen first. A web page can't see or undo Night Shift, True Tone or colour filters, and they all tint colours warm.

  • Night Shift & True Tone: Control Centre → long-press brightness, toggle both off.
  • Brightness: turn it up; dim screens mute colours.
  • Also check Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size: Colour Filters and Reduce White Point should be off.

Not sure? Tap White check. It should look pure white, not cream or orange.

Colours were sampled from a photo of my physical House of Colour swatch and colour-corrected, so they're close, not certified. On wide-gamut screens they're shown in Display P3.
