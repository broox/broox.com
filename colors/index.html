Mid Summer

Summer colors are: soft, blue, smokey, and rose

House of Colour · tap a color for full screen

For true colors, check your screen first. A web page can't see or undo Night Shift, True Tone or color filters, and they all tint colors warm.

  • Night Shift & True Tone: Control Center → long-press brightness, toggle both off.
  • Brightness: turn it up; dim screens mute colors.
  • Also check Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size: Color Filters and Reduce White Point should be off.

Not sure? Tap White check. It should look pure white, not cream or orange.
